# Automatic architecture film

Experience mode, inheriting the approved monochrome SF Pro design and diamond-loop mark. User selected the continuous architecture film after invoking Impeccable Overdrive. One persistent 3,400px architecture world contains 14 scenes, animated by a spring camera. All demonstrations are timed; speakers talk without operating them. Start once; pause, timeline and scene controls are rehearsal/contingency tools. Six configurable speaking slots preserve the exact sequence: Diljot intro, Ivan engines, Adarsh rules, Shambavi audit, Anubhav handoffs/manager, Diljot architecture/future. Default runtime is 8 minutes 38 seconds.

The animation owns nearly the whole viewport. No subtitle paragraphs, large side headings or metric walls. Detailed explanation lives in notes. Document layers unfold, OCR traverses regions, entities assemble, destructive masks form, traceability records assemble and decisions automatically demonstrate pending/approve/deny. Existing architecture elements never disappear between shots. Diljot's final section springs backward into the entire system, then reveals proposed network/share branches.

Synthetic examples and demonstrations are labeled. No actual redaction requests, policy edits or decisions run from this presentation. Current pending/outage availability and explicit-denial behavior are represented accurately. Network enforcement and shared-conversation interception are future scope. Existing public test portrait is bundled for the visual demonstration; no private document or real employee data is bundled.

Readability, grayscale, reduced motion, local/offline use and continuous camera transformation are required. The website remains static, independent of the app/backend.

Motion blur follows camera screen velocity (bounded to 5.5px), clearing at rest. Local handoff, layer unfolding, classification/evidence assembly and shared-result motion have matching transient blur. Reduced-motion mode removes blur, camera interpolation, typing, caret blinking, packet travel and source rocking; semantic stages still advance. Mobile buttons retain at least 44px targets.
